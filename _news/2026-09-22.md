Our collaboration with Angela Oliverio's lab is out in *Cell*: [A geothermal amoeba sets a new upper temperature limit for eukaryotes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.043). Jess built an orthology assignment pipeline on protein language models that assigns orthologs across highly divergent species. This let us compare protein structure and function pair by pair in this heat-tolerant amoeba, and find unique physical-chemical properties that we think let it survive at 63°C. The work was covered by [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/22/science/worlds-hottest-amoeba.html) and many other outlets, including [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/22/nx-s1-5977044/fire-amoeba-extremophile-eukaryote-life) and [Science News](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/fire-amoeba-record-breaking-heat). Syracuse University wrote it up: [Biologists Discover 'Fire Amoeba' that Pushes the Limits of Complex Life](https://news.syr.edu/2026/10/01/biologists-discover-fire-amoeba-that-pushes-the-limits-of-complex-life/).
