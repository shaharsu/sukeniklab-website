Our work with Angela Oliverio's lab on a record-breaking amoeba is out in [*Cell*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.043)! Jess built a protein language model pipeline to assign orthologs across highly divergent species, which let us find unique physical-chemical properties that we think help it survive at 63°C. Covered by [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/22/science/worlds-hottest-amoeba.html) and many other outlets.
