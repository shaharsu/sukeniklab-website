Congratulations to Kara, Trevor, Kavindu and Lea, and to lab alumna Karina Guadalupe, on the publication of [our GOOSE paper in *Nature*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10849-1)! Kara and Trevor share first authorship on the work, which introduces a framework for designing disordered proteins to order so that sequence-to-function questions can be asked systematically. Syracuse University wrote it up: [A&S Scientists Design Proteins With No Fixed Shape](https://news.syr.edu/2026/08/05/as-scientists-design-proteins-with-no-fixed-shape/).
