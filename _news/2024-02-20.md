Our fearless PI, Professor Shahar Sukenik, is UC Merced's first recipient of the Alfred P. Sloan foundation's Sloan Research Fellowship. He is one of only 126 recipients across the US and Canada. Shahar credits his success to the amazing grad students who helped him build the lab. It is a very well-deserved award. We are so proud of you, Shahar!
